What HallaSport includes

HallaSport

HallaSport brings team schedules, messages and payments together. Players and guardians see what is coming up, and coaches know who will attend.

Calendar

Players and guardians can check practice and match times and venues, then confirm attendance. Coaches can see who is coming and record who attended.

Messages

Has the venue changed or is the match starting early? Players and guardians can find the coach’s instructions in team messages and club-wide announcements in club news.

Payments

Clubs can bill season fees through HallaSport. Guardians and players can see at a glance which fees are outstanding and which have been paid.

Teams

Clubs set up teams for each season and assign players, coaches and other staff. Each team’s details show who plays and who coaches.

Statistics

How is the season going for you and your team? Statistics can show how many training sessions you’ve attended and matches you’ve played, alongside team results and league position. Depending on the sport, they can also include minutes played, goals and assists.

Club website

Clubs get a website template to introduce their teams and explain how to join. New players can find a suitable team and its contact person before their first practice.

Fan shop

HallaSport provides a ready-to-use club shop where fans can buy merchandise and match tickets.

Integrations

HallaSport can be connected to the services your club uses. Possible integrations include

  • importing fixtures from a federation into the team calendar
  • showing training sessions and matches in family calendars
  • sending season fee and sales data to the club’s accounting software
  • publishing events and results on the club website
HallaBit

HallaSport is developed by HallaBit, a Finnish software company. Clubs can tell the developers directly what works and where they would like improvements.

Users

Guardian

Guardians can check when their child’s next practice is and confirm whether their child will attend. They can see which season fees are due or paid and send a message to the coach.

Coach

Coaches plan training sessions and see who is coming. At practice they record attendance. If the venue changes, they can let the team know.

Administrator

The administrator creates teams, invites members and reviews access requests. They manage seasons and publish club news. Other roles include player, team manager, team treasurer and club treasurer.

Club homepage

New players can explore the club’s teams before their first practice. Fans can read club news, check when the next home game starts and follow the link to buy tickets.
